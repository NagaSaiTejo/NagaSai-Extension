# Privacy Policy for NagaSai AI

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at NagaSai AI ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

When you use our Chrome Extension, NagaSai AI (the "Extension"), we appreciate that you are trusting us with your information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information is processed, how it is used, and what rights you have in relation to it.

Please read this privacy notice carefully as it will help you understand what we do with the information that is processed by the Extension.

## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

**In short:** *We, as the developers of NagaSai AI, do not collect, store, or sell any of your personal data on our servers. All data processing happens locally on your device or is sent directly to your chosen AI providers.*

To function properly, the Extension processes the following information locally on your device:
*   **Authentication Information:** If you use "Guest Mode" and provide your own API keys (e.g., for OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Groq), these keys are stored strictly locally in your browser's local storage (or synced via Chrome Sync if you have it enabled). We never transmit these keys to our own servers.
*   **Website Content & Screenshots:** When you ask the AI to summarize or analyze a webpage, the Extension extracts the text content (DOM) of the active tab. If you use the Camera feature, it captures a screenshot of the active tab. This data is **only** captured when you explicitly initiate a request.
*   **Personal Communications:** Your chat history with the AI assistant is saved locally in your browser so you can review previous conversations.

## 2. How Is Your Information Shared?

**In short:** *Your data is only shared with the third-party AI providers you interact with.*

Because NagaSai AI acts as a bridge between your browser and Large Language Models (LLMs), the Website Content, Screenshots, and Chat Prompts you submit are transmitted directly to the APIs of the AI providers (such as Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or Pollinations.ai) to generate a response. 

We do not act as a middleman. Your data is sent directly from your browser to these providers. The use of your data by these providers is governed by their respective Privacy Policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the AI models you choose to use.

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or browsing data to any third parties.**

## 3. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

**In short:** *We do not keep your information. You control your data.*

Because all data (chat history, API keys, preferences) is stored locally on your device, it is kept only for as long as you choose to keep it. You can delete your chat history or remove your API keys at any time directly through the Extension's settings or by clearing your browser's local storage. Uninstalling the Extension will also remove this local data.

## 4. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

**In short:** *We rely on Chrome's secure storage mechanisms and secure HTTPS connections.*

Your API keys and chat history are secured using Chrome's native `chrome.storage` API. When the Extension communicates with third-party AI providers, it does so exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections, ensuring your prompts and website data are secure in transit.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Effective Date" and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
